Company

Rigorous analysis, practical delivery.

MZE Digital (Pvt) Limited is a data science, analytics and software company for finance and capital markets, registered with the SECP and FBR in Pakistan and operating across Singapore, Seoul and Karachi.

Who we are

Built on two decades of market and data experience.

MZE Digital was founded by Ertiza Abbas, a quantitative data and macro strategic analyst with 20 years of experience across business development, economic strategy and analytics.

We exist to close the gap between analysis and action: to turn market and business data into tested models, clear analytics and dependable software. Alongside our core data and software work, we offer coaching, professional training and publishing.

Our services Meet the team

Mission

Give finance teams quantitative clarity through data science, analytics and software.

Vision

Institutions that decide with clean data, tested models and well-trained teams.

Approach

Evidence first: out-of-sample testing, real-world costs and transparent assumptions.

Integrity

Neutral, consistent analysis, with the same standard applied to every market and state.

Our principle

Good analysis is not the most complicated model. It is the honest one: tested out of sample, with every real-world cost counted.
