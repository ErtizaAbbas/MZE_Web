Company

Rigorous analysis, practical delivery.

MZE Digital (Pvt) Limited is a consultancy, coaching, training and technology company registered with the SECP and FBR in Pakistan, operating across Singapore, Seoul and Karachi.

Who we are

Built on two decades of market and strategy experience.

MZE Digital was founded by Ertiza Abbas, a quantitative data and macro strategic analyst with 20 years of experience across business development, economic strategy and analytics.

We exist to close the gap between analysis and action: to turn rigorous research into better decisions, skilled people and working systems. Our work spans strategy consultancy, coaching and mentorship, professional training, technology and publishing, and we are the parent company behind KSE Sentinel.
