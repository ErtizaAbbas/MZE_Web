01

Data Science & Machine Learning

Predictive models built for financial data: noisy, non-stationary and expensive to get wrong.

We design, validate and document models that forecast returns, volatility and risk, and we test them the way markets will: out of sample, walk-forward, with realistic costs.

What we do

  • Return, volatility and risk forecasting
  • Regression and regularised models (OLS, Ridge)
  • Feature engineering from price, volume and macro data
  • Walk-forward and out-of-sample validation

Typical deliverables

Validated modelsResearch notebooksModel documentation

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Clear, decision-ready views of markets, portfolios and business performance.

From one-off analysis to always-on dashboards, we turn raw market and business data into reporting that leadership, desks and clients can act on.

What we do

  • Portfolio and performance analytics
  • Market breadth, sector rotation and heatmaps
  • KPI dashboards and automated reports
  • Ad-hoc analysis and research support

Typical deliverables

Interactive dashboardsAutomated reportsAnalysis packs

Software Development for Finance

Institutional-grade platforms, APIs and tools for trading, research and risk.

We build and maintain production software for finance, from multi-tenant analytics platforms to focused internal tools, with the same engineering behind KSE Sentinel.

What we do

  • Trading and analytics platforms

  • APIs and integrations (Python, FastAPI)
  • Alert engines, watchlists and position-sizing tools

Typical deliverables

Production softwareAPIsMaintenance & support
04

Data Engineering & Market Data

Reliable pipelines that deliver clean, timely market data without manual work.

Good models start with good data. We automate collection, cleaning and storage so analysts spend their time on analysis, not on copying files.

What we do

  • Automated end-of-day data ingestion
  • Report and PDF extraction into structured data
  • Data cleaning, validation and quality checks
  • Scheduled jobs and queryable data stores

Typical deliverables

Data pipelinesClean datasetsRunbooks
05

Quantitative Research & Backtesting

Systematic strategy research for capital markets, with every real-world cost counted.

We research, test and stress-test systematic strategies across equities, indices, FX, commodities and crypto, and report results you can trust.

What we do

  • Signal research (trend, momentum, mean reversion)
  • Broker-friction-adjusted backtesting
  • ATR-based risk and position sizing
  • Robustness and cost stress tests

Typical deliverables

Strategy research reportsBacktest frameworksSignal libraries

Capital Markets & Strategy Advisory

Macro, market and geopolitical analysis for organisations making high-stakes decisions.

We combine quantitative evidence with macro and strategic analysis to give leadership teams a structured view of markets, risks and scenarios.

What we do

  • Macro and market outlooks
  • Geopolitical risk modelling and scenarios
  • Data and analytics strategy
  • Executive briefings

Typical deliverables

Briefing packsScenario frameworksData strategy roadmaps
